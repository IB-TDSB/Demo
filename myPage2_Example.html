On March 29, 2025, the Moon will
pass in front of and partially block
the Sun, casting a shadow on parts of the
Northern Hemisphere. The central part of the
Moon’s shadow, where the Sun would appear
completely blocked, misses Earth, so no one
will be able to see a total solar eclipse this
time.

Everyone watching the eclipse must
use proper eye protection or an indirect
viewing method to protect their eyes. (See more
information on safety below.)
